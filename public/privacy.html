Privacy Policy

NeatClock is built to collect as little as possible. Here's exactly what exists.

Your schedule stays on your device

NeatClock has no backend, no database, and no user accounts. The tasks, presets, and theme you configure are stored only in your browser's localStorage — they are never transmitted to any server. Clearing your browser data removes them. Exported .ics and backup .json files are generated entirely in your browser and download directly to your device.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect or store any personal data or cross-site identifiers. It reports aggregate page views and a small number of named product events (for example, "exported a calendar"), with no way to trace any event back to an individual visitor.

The feedback form

If you choose to submit the optional "what should we build next" feedback form, your answers (schedule type, interests, purchase intent, and an email address only if you provide one) are sent to Formspree, a third-party form backend, and land in our inbox. We use this solely to gauge interest in optional paid extras. We do not sell or share this data. If you provided an email and want it removed, contact us and we'll delete it from our inbox.

Affiliate links

Some exported calendar events for common household or vehicle tasks include an Amazon affiliate link. See our affiliate disclosure for details. Clicking these links does not send us any information about you — Amazon's own privacy policy governs what happens once you're on amazon.com.

Cookies

NeatClock does not set any cookies. localStorage is not a cookie — it's never sent to any server and is only readable by neatclock.pro.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the feedback form in the app footer, or open an issue on GitHub.
